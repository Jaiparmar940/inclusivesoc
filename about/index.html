About Us

We deliver innovative & non-traditional solutions to local and global issues

Our Story

Solutions rooted in creativity

Founded in 2019, we have worked on projects addressing local inequities to a global lack of resources. We are comprised of mostly college students with diverse academic backgrounds and upbringings.

Our solutions are rooted in creativity. Whether sourcing chemical compounds for delivering clean water across the globe or instilling STEM futures for youth, we strive to pioneer focused solutions to specific issues.

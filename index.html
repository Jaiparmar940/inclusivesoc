FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Who are you?
We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization focused on employing innovative approaches to democratize resources to address local and global inequities. We believe that by taking on a creative philosophy, we can foster a more collective, more inclusive society.
What areas do you serve?
As our projects are highly variable in their goals, the areas we serve tend to vary greatly. At the moment, we are focusing efforts in aiding communities across North America, South America, and sub-Saharan Africa. We do not focus on any one particular region, and we don't plan to!
Can I donate towards a specific project?
Yes! At the donation page, you will have the option to select which project(s) you wish to contribute your funds to. If none are selected, donated funds will be dispersed towards charitable causes at the discretion of Inclusive Society.
How can I contribute?
If you are an individual and you wish to support our cause, you may consider donating here. If you have additional suggestions, comments, or would like a hands-on role, feel free to contact us here. For organizations interested in partnerships, please contact us at this page.
What do you support?
Our causes vary substantially. Currently, we are focused on providing aid to the citizens of Palestine. We have also done missions for democratizing access to STEM tools, clean water, and medical technology. The common factor with our current and future projects is our pursuit to address widespread inequities.
Are donations tax deductible?
Yes! We maintain a 501(c)(3) federal status, so all donations are considered tax deductible except where prohibited by law.
Where can I contact a specific board member?
Go to the about us page to contact a specific member.
Question not answered above?
Contact us — we're more than happy to help, or email us at info@inclusivesoc.org.
